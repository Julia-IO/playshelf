Beat

Tap the squares to add drum hits. The 4 bright numbers are the beats you'd clap along to. Tip: Kick on 1 and 3, Snare on 2 and 4 = a classic beat!

Bass

The bass is the deep, rumbly part that makes people dance. All notes fit together, so you can't make a wrong one!

Melody

Pick an instrument from any style, then draw your tune. Higher rows = higher notes. Try going up and down like stairs.

Your Voice

Record yourself singing, rapping, beatboxing or saying something funny (up to 8 seconds). Then tap the grid to choose when it plays in your song.

The microphone is blocked on this page. No problem! Record yourself with the Voice Memos / Recorder app on a phone or computer, then press Upload and pick the recording.

Tracks

Build your whole song here. Record your loop or your voice into tracks, stack them so they play together, then move, cut and stretch them to any length.

The microphone is blocked on this page. Record yourself with the Voice Memos / Recorder app on a phone or computer, then press Upload to put the recording on a track.

Mixer

Make every part sit just right. Volume = loudness. M mutes a track, S plays only that track. Pan moves it left or right, Echo repeats it, Space makes it sound like a big hall.

Record your song

If your Tracks timeline has clips, it records the whole timeline. If not, your loop plays 4 times. Then you can listen back and save it.
